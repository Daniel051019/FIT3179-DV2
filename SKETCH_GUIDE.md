# Draw your required sketch by hand

Use this as a planning guide. The submitted sketch must be your own hand-drawn A4 sketch; this Markdown document is not a substitute. The brief awards zero sketch marks for a sketch created with digital tools.

Draw a portrait A4 page as a long scrolling webpage, with five clearly labelled chapters. Use ruler lines and brief notes rather than copying all the website text. If you already submitted a sketch, use that as the authoritative layout and revise this page to follow it; do not claim a newly drawn sketch was previously submitted.

1. **Header and overview:** title, one-sentence purpose, author/date, and three large figures ($77,000 salary, $40,500 study-area spread, 4.7% gender gap). Mark the audience: Malaysian students interested in Australian graduate work.
2. **Study area:** a wide horizontal salary bar chart with 21 rows, descending 2025 order, an orange $77,000 reference line and a year selector. Below it, sketch text beside six small bullet rows.
3. **Finding work:** a wide scatterplot (full-time employment rate on x, salary on y) with national reference lines and hover tooltips. Then draw three parallel axes (salary, full-time employment, wanting more hours) connected by three study-area lines. Below, a 10 × 10 waffle beside an occupation treemap. Then a two-column heatmap for part-time work and wanting more hours. Write the two employment denominators next to the waffle/scatter charts.
4. **Gender:** draw connected female/male dots for each study area, using orange/teal. Below, a diverging bar chart for the gender gap. Note that two fields have suppressed gender values and are omitted from paired comparisons.
5. **Over time:** draw the two salary lines (reported dollars and 2025 dollars), then a four-column waterfall beside the four-field 2024–25 salary slope chart. Annotate the small 1.9% real gain.
6. **Place:** mark this as **wider labour-market context, not state graduate starting salaries**. Draw a choropleth for all-employee weekly earnings beside a proportional-symbol map for bachelor-degree-holder counts. Below, put explanatory text beside a tile cartogram for qualification shares. Label the projection on the two geographic maps and the schematic nature of the tiles.
7. **Footer:** takeaway, data sources, definitions, author/date and AI acknowledgement.

Use 2–3 restrained colours, show alignment and whitespace, and write the major x/y labels and legends. The finished sketch should show chart variety and page grouping, not just blank rectangles labelled “chart”. Photograph it evenly in good light, crop the paper edges, and check the labels are legible in the PDF.

Discuss the bullet chart and tile cartogram with your tutor when reviewing the planned advanced idioms. The final webpage should follow your accepted sketch and feedback.
